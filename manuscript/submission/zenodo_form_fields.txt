Description
    Benchmark harness, per-method adapters, run configurations, environment
    lockfiles, result tables and figure scripts for the preprint "Annotating
    single-cell data on a laptop: a benchmark of annotation methods and
    practical low-memory workflows, with actinn-jax".

    This archive is the source tree at release v1.0.0, commit 15c9cae, which is
    the state that produced every number and figure in that manuscript.

    The archive is code and derived result tables. The pre-trained references it
    builds on are deposited separately at doi:10.5281/zenodo.21688150. The six
    benchmark datasets are third-party (HLiCA, HLCA, Krasnow, IBDverse, pbmc3k
    and the CELLxGENE Census) and are cited in the paper rather than redeposited.
